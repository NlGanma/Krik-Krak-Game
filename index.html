A ROOM BEFORE MORNING

Take your time.

Explore from a child’s point of view. Jump onto the bed, suitcase, and furniture to find your own route. When someone pounds on the door, reach the light switch beside it and put the light out. Stay in the dark for two seconds and they will give up; bring the light back too soon and they return. You have ten seconds before the door gives way.
